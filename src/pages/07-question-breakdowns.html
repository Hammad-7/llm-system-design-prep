LLM System Design · Fast Track

Question Breakdowns

Complete walkthroughs of the most common LLM system design prompts, following the delivery framework from requirements to deep dives, with what's expected at each level.

Each breakdown is written the way a strong candidate would run the interview: requirements first, then entities and API, then a high-level design built one requirement at a time, then deep dives. In the deep dives you'll see three options for each problem:

  • Bad Solution what many candidates propose first, and why it breaks.
  • Good Solution a solid answer that passes at mid-level.
  • Great Solution what senior and staff candidates reach, usually by quantifying or by handling failure modes.

Pick the breakdown closest to your interview type and try it cold first (see How to Prepare).
